Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

Thread Haus is committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the confidentiality of the personal information provided during any interaction or activity on our website. The purpose of this Privacy Policy is to inform you how Thread Haus processes your personal information when you visit our website, browse products or contact us with an order inquiry.

All personal information shall be processed in accordance with the Philippine data protection laws, including Republic Act No. 10173 (also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012) and its implementing rules and regulations.

2. Personal Information We Collect

Thread Haus may collect your personal information that you voluntarily give when contacting us or making an order inquiry. Depending on the type of interaction with us, the personal information includes:

  • Contact information: your name, email address, and other contact details that you decide to provide;
  • Order information: the selected T-shirt, its size, quantity and other details necessary to process your order inquiry;
  • Delivery information: your shipping address and other delivery details, if such information is necessary for fulfillment of your confirmed order;
  • Communication information: information contained in your emails, questions, feedback and other communications;
  • Records of communication: data from emails, inquiries, feedback, and any other messages that you communicate to us;
  • Technical data: limited data related to your device, browser, or usage of the website, collected through the website's hosting provider or technologies used.

The company only collects the data that is necessary for the above-described purposes or otherwise is required by the law.

3. Usage of Your Data

The company can use personal data for the following purposes:

  • Handling inquiries related to the company's products and services.
  • Processing and verifying orders and inquiries.
  • Providing information about availability, cost, shipping details, and delivering updates.
  • Order fulfillment and customer support.
  • Website maintenance and security.
  • Resolving any complaints and disputes and keeping relevant business records.
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Personal data will be processed solely on the grounds of an appropriate lawful basis. When consent is required by the law, it will be obtained.

4. Order Inquiries and Communication

The customers can submit inquiries about our products and potential purchases via the website. Personal data shared during the process can be used to respond to the inquiries.

Sending an inquiry does not necessarily mean confirmation and acceptance of an order. Additional information concerning a potential purchase may be provided as necessary. Thread Haus manages all inquiries and corresponding communication in relation to its Privacy Policy and applicable law.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Thread Haus does not sell personal information to any third parties. Personal information may be shared to the extent reasonably required for legitimate business purposes or where legally required or permitted. Depending on the situation, such information may be disclosed to:

  • Website and email providers: for website hosting, email communications, and other technical services.
  • Delivery providers: for arranging shipping and delivering confirmed orders, where applicable.
  • Professional or business service providers: where required for conducting legitimate business and in accordance with appropriate safeguards.
  • Government authorities or other third parties: where disclosure is required by law or is needed in lawful proceedings.

Reasonable efforts are made to ensure that personal information shared with service providers is handled appropriately and securely from misuse or unauthorized disclosure.

6. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

Personal information may be stored in email accounts, communications systems, or business records related to customer inquiries and orders. Thread Haus takes reasonable steps to protect the Personal Information against any form of unauthorized access, disclosure, manipulation, loss, or destruction. We keep Personal Information as long as it is reasonably required to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with applicable laws, resolve any dispute, or preserve business records.

Upon completion of such purposes, Thread Haus takes reasonable measures to delete, destroy, or render unidentifiable the Personal Information, where appropriate. Even though we undertake reasonable measures to protect the Personal Information, no means of electronic transmission or storage of the data is completely secure.

7. Cookies and Other Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the configuration of the website platform or website hosting service or other third-party tools employed by us.

Such technologies, when actually implemented on our website, may help to perform various functions related to website functionality, security, or performance. Users may control the use of cookies through their browsers' settings. Thread Haus does not represent that it uses analytics, advertising trackers, or other tracking technologies unless they are actually implemented on our website.

8. Links to Other Sites and Services

Links to third-party sites or services may appear on our website for ease or convenience. These third-party services operate independently and may have their own privacy policies or methods for handling personal information.

Thread Haus does not control their policies and is not responsible for their content, security, or privacy policies. It is recommended that visitors examine the privacy policies before providing any personal information on third-party websites or services.

9. Your Rights Under Philippine Data Privacy Law

Subject to applicable law, you may be able to exercise the following rights:

  • Be informed of the collection and processing of your personal information.
  • Request access to personal information relating to you subject to applicable conditions.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Object to processing of your personal information for certain purposes where such is allowed by applicable law.
  • Request blocking, erasing, or destruction of your personal information for legal reasons.
  • Request appropriate remedy or compensation if available under the law.
  • File a complaint to the National Privacy Commission if you believe that there has been a violation of your data privacy rights.

These rights are subject to applicable legal requirements, exceptions and limitations. Please reach Thread Haus through the contact information provided on our website for exercising any right or raising privacy concerns.

10. Children's Privacy

The Thread Haus website is intended for customers who want to purchase T-shirts. We do not intend to collect children's personal information without any authorized or lawful reasons. If there is any belief that children have supplied any personal information, please let us know so we can investigate and act appropriately according to the law.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Thread Haus may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time due to any changes in our website, business practices or legal requirements. The updated Privacy Policy will be published on this page along with its new effective date. Visitors are advised to regularly check this policy. Revisions will be implemented according to the law and will not strip visitors of any right that cannot legally be waived or restricted.

12. Contact Us
